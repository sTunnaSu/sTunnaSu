# Frozen provenance definitions

```text
observed_at
Time represented by the observation itself.

Examples:
- exchange event timestamp
- quote timestamp
- historical bar-close timestamp


source_capture_at
Time AgentLarry's data adapter received,
downloaded or exposed the observation
to the strategy.


decision_at
Time the strategy generated the decision.


data_age_ms
decision_at - observed_at


ingestion_delay_ms
source_capture_at - observed_at


processing_delay_ms
decision_at - source_capture_at
```

For historical OHLCV, describe the result as **simulated bar-level execution delay**, not exchange-grade microstructure latency.
